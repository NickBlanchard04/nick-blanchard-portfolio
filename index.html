Frequently asked questions

What to know before we start.

Clear answers about scope, timing, launch, and support before you send an inquiry.

What happens after I inquire?

Send me your business or program name, website goal, pages or features, target timing, one or two websites you like, and your best contact method. I review each inquiry personally. We’ll confirm fit and scope, then I’ll send a clear quote before any work begins.

How long does a website take?

We’ll confirm timing when we clarify the scope. Include your target timing in your inquiry so we can discuss it before work begins.

What is included in a website package?

Every package includes strategy, custom responsive design, development, clear page structure, a contact path, Google-ready setup, mobile and desktop testing, launch, and a practical handoff. Essentials includes up to 4 pages, Growth up to 7, and Custom up to 10 plus one simple booking-request workflow.

What does “Google-ready” mean?

It means the technical foundation is in place: SEO basics, page metadata, a sitemap, and indexing setup. This helps search engines understand the site; it does not guarantee rankings, traffic, or leads.

Is website care required?

No. Care is optional after launch. Technical Care is $99/month for technical upkeep and routine site maintenance. Care + Edits is $179/month and adds up to 60 minutes of website edits each month.

How do revisions work?

We review and refine the website before launch, within the scope confirmed in your quote. New pages or features beyond that scope are additional work.

What if I need work beyond a package?

Additions beyond a package start at $110/hour with a $165 minimum. Any third-party costs are separate.

What is not included?

Domain, hosting, booking platforms, paid tools, and other third-party costs are separate from the website package. Work beyond the package scope is priced separately.
