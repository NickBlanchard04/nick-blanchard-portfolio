Local service business Green Wave Landscaping

A direct route from first look to getting in touch.

I designed and built a focused service site around three practical steps: understand the services, review the work, and start a quote request.

View live website ↗
Project goal
Create a direct, mobile-friendly path from learning about the work to requesting a quote.
Audience
Prospective local customers comparing lawn mowing, patio work, or landscaping and ready to call or text.
Scope
Custom HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; responsive layouts; three service categories; a filterable project gallery; lightbox; and call or text actions.
Design and build decisions
I paired each photo-led service with a direct quote action, then connected it to a categorized gallery so visitors can move from an offering to relevant examples without leaving the page.
Shipped outcome
The live site lets visitors scan three services, review 13 categorized project images, and start a call or prefilled quote text without working through a form.
Sports organization Upstate Basketball League

One digital home for a growing basketball league.

As UBL’s founder, I designed and built the public league site and the update workflow behind it—one system for league information and one destination for families and programs.

View live website ↗
Project goal
Build a trustworthy public home for a new faith-rooted varsity league before its inaugural season, with core league information that can be maintained consistently.
Audience
Student-athletes and families, coaches and athletic directors, and schools or independent programs evaluating the league.
Scope
Information architecture, visual system, responsive static frontend, league-data workflow, accessibility and search foundations, quality assurance, and launch.
Design and build decisions
I used a sports-editorial system while keeping schedules and standings faster to scan than the decoration. One validated data model supplies the homepage, schedule, standings, ticker, and brackets, with live and offline fallbacks.
Shipped outcome
The custom-domain site is live before the inaugural 2026–27 season, with public team, schedule, standings, bracket, rules, news, sponsorship, and league-information pages plus a tested release path.

Project status
Concept Project · Industry Demonstration · Not a client project. Pepper Window is fictional and is not an operating restaurant.
Concept brief
Explore how a neighborhood restaurant identity could carry from visual direction into an expressive, responsive website without presenting invented work as a real commission.
Scope
Self-directed identity, original concept imagery, responsive HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, menu filtering, an in-memory order preview, a table-finder interaction, accessibility review, and browser QA.
Design and build decisions
I paired bundled display type, heavy rules, interrupted frames, and direct food imagery with repeated disclosures. Ordering and table controls remain browser-only previews with no checkout, backend, analytics, personal fields, or submission.
Experience shown
The published prototype shows a complete restaurant-site direction while visibly separating fiction from fact at the portfolio card, sticky disclosure rail, interactions, footer, metadata, and structured data.

Explore the concept →
Project status
Concept Project · Industry Demonstration · Not a client project. Daymark Dental House is fictional and is not a real dental practice, provider, or location.
Concept brief
Demonstrate how a calm family-dental direction could carry from identity through a complete responsive experience without presenting the work as a real commission or operating practice.
Scope
Self-directed identity, original concept imagery, responsive HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, flippable service cards, fictional team and case-study content, a browser-only booking demonstration, accessibility review, and browser QA.
Design and safeguards
Persistent concept labeling, visible fictional-image captions, and explicit no-provider and no-outcome language keep the status clear. The booking demonstration uses no named form controls, network requests, analytics, or storage.
Demonstrated experience
Visitors can explore interface patterns and local validation with invented details only. No availability is checked, no appointment is created, and no information is transmitted or saved.

Frequently asked questions

What to know before we start.

Clear answers about scope, timing, launch, and support before you send an inquiry.

What happens after I inquire?

Send me your business or program name, website goal, pages or features, target timing, one or two websites you like, and your best contact method. I review each inquiry personally. We’ll confirm fit and scope, then I’ll send a clear quote before any work begins.

How long does a website take?

We’ll confirm timing when we clarify the scope. Include your target timing in your inquiry so we can discuss it before work begins.

What is included in a website package?

Every package includes strategy, custom responsive design, development, clear page structure, a contact path, Google-ready setup, mobile and desktop testing, launch, and a practical handoff. Essentials includes up to 4 pages, Growth up to 7, and Custom up to 10 plus one simple booking-request workflow.

What does “Google-ready” mean?

It means the technical foundation is in place: SEO basics, page metadata, a sitemap, and indexing setup. This helps search engines understand the site; it does not guarantee rankings, traffic, or leads.

Is website care required?

No. Care is optional after launch. Technical Care is $99/month for technical upkeep and routine site maintenance. Care + Edits is $179/month and adds up to 60 minutes of website edits each month.

How do revisions work?

We review and refine the website before launch, within the scope confirmed in your quote. New pages or features beyond that scope are additional work.

What if I need work beyond a package?

Additions beyond a package start at $110/hour with a $165 minimum. Any third-party costs are separate.

What is not included?

Domain, hosting, booking platforms, paid tools, and other third-party costs are separate from the website package. Work beyond the package scope is priced separately.
