Common Questions

Everything you need to know

How does the process start?

Start with a short inquiry. I learn about your business, your goals, and what the website should help people do. Then I outline the next step.

What happens on the call?

I ask about your business, audience, current site, goals, and timing. You leave with a clear direction and no pressure to decide on the spot.

Do you only build the site, or manage it too?

I can build the site as a focused project with a clean handoff, then provide optional ongoing care if you want help keeping it current.

How do you help with SEO and AI search visibility?

I set up clear page structure, metadata, sitemap support, performance basics, and useful content. That helps discovery systems understand the site; it does not guarantee rankings.

How fast can a site go live?

Timing depends on the number of pages, the features involved, and how quickly the content is ready. I confirm a realistic schedule with you before the project starts.

Can you improve an existing site, or do you rebuild from scratch?

Both are possible. I refine solid foundations and recommend a rebuild when it will create a clearer, faster, easier-to-maintain result.

What kinds of businesses work with Built by Blanch?

Local service businesses, professional practices, organizations, shops, restaurants, and growing teams that need a clearer online presence.

How do you turn traffic into leads?

I pair clear information and credible proof with an obvious next step, so interested visitors can understand the business and contact you easily.
