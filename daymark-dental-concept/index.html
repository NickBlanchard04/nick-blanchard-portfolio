About the concept

The essentials, without making you search the fine print.

Is Daymark Dental House a real practice?

No. Daymark is an original fictional brand created by Built by Blanch as a dental-industry portfolio demonstration. It is not a client or healthcare provider.

Are the clinical cases real?

No. Every case narrative and visual is illustrative. No patient record, testimonial, or treatment result is represented.

What happens when I submit the form?

The interface displays a demo confirmation in your browser. It does not contact a practice, save your entries, or make an appointment.
