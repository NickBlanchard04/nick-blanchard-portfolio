Privacy Policy

Effective: July 31, 2026

Built by Blanch is operated by Nick Blanchard. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit builtbyblanch.com or ask about a project, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

You may voluntarily provide information including:

  • Name and contact details
  • Business or organization name
  • Website or domain information
  • Project goals, timing, and message

This information may arrive through email, a website form, a call, or another conversation you begin.

This information may be collected through:

  • Project inquiry forms
  • Email communications

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Reply to inquiries and prepare project plans

  • Improve the website and diagnose technical issues

Built by Blanch does not sell or rent personal information.

Third-Party Services

Information may be handled by services that support the site or a project, such as:

  • Website hosting and security providers

  • Payment processors used for an agreed project

These providers process information under their own privacy terms.

Cookies & Analytics

The site may use cookies or privacy-limited analytics to understand general traffic and improve the experience. You can limit cookies in your browser settings.

Data Security

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where allowed

Email Built by Blanch to make a request.

Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
