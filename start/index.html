Project basics

Common questions

Everything you need to know.

How is pricing handled?

Pricing depends on page count, content readiness, and the features the project needs. You receive a clear written scope and quote before work begins.

How long does a build take?

Timing varies with scope and feedback. We agree on a realistic schedule before the project starts rather than promising a one-size-fits-all deadline.

Can you improve an existing site?

Yes. I can refine a solid foundation or recommend a rebuild when that will produce a clearer and more maintainable result.

Do I have to manage the code?

No. The handoff is practical, and optional ongoing care can cover technical upkeep or a defined block of future changes.

What makes the process different?

The strategy, design, and development stay with one person, so decisions remain connected and communication stays direct.

What businesses are a good fit?

Local services, professional practices, organizations, shops, restaurants, and growing businesses that need a clearer online presence.
